# Food-Waste-Tracker-for-Hostel-Mess
# AN OVERVIEW
This is a python based project that will help track the food wastage across the hostels in the college campus by tracking food prepared, consumed and help analyze the amount of food wasted.
# Why I chose this project
The issue of food wastage in hostel mess came to my attention during a discussion in our environmental sustainability class, which led me to notice the amount of food being wasted each day in the mess. This project will help in tracking the food wastage and analyzing the trends could help in reducing the food wastage potentially.
# Features
- record food prepared (names and quantities)
- record food wasted(names and quantities)
- calculate the amount and percentage of food wasted
- view and manage previous records
- analyze wastage based on food items prepared
- generate reports and suggestions
